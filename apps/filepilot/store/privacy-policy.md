## 1. Summary
FilePilot processes your images and PDF documents **on your device**. We operate no server for file processing and never receive your files, their names or their contents.

## 2. Files you process
- You choose each file in Android's system picker. FilePilot receives a temporary private copy; your original is only read, never modified.
- Processing (compression, resizing, conversion, PDF merge/split/extraction, image↔PDF) happens locally.
- Temporary copies are deleted when you leave a tool and at every app start. Results are stored in the app's private storage until you delete them, or copied to a folder you choose.
- Shared files are given to the app you pick through a temporary, read-only Android content link.
- Output images do not retain the original EXIF metadata (including GPS location).

## 3. Data stored on your device
Operation history (type, file names, sizes, dates, status), preferences and a cached Premium status are stored locally in SQLite. They never leave your device. You can clear history or delete all data in Settings; uninstalling removes everything. Android cloud backup is disabled for FilePilot.

## 4. Advertising (free version)
We use Google AdMob, which may process your device's advertising ID and coarse technical data to show and measure ads. Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform and can be changed in Settings. Ads have no access to your files. Premium users see no ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 5. Purchases
Payments are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not your files.

## 6. Analytics (optional)
Off by default. If enabled, anonymous product events (e.g. "compression completed", format, size bucket) may be collected. They never include file names, file contents or document metadata.

## 7. Children
FilePilot is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

## 8. Your rights
You may access, delete or object to processing of data described above. Contact: [EMAIL]. EU/UK users may lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

## 9. Changes
We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
