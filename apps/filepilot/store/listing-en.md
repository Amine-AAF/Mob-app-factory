# FilePilot — Google Play listing (en-US)

**App name (30 max):** FilePilot - Image & PDF Tools

**Short description (80 max):**
Compress, resize, convert images and manage PDF files offline.

**Full description (4000 max):**

FilePilot is a lightweight, privacy-first toolbox for the images and PDF files you work with every day. Every operation runs directly on your phone: no account, no upload, and it works offline.

**Image tools**
• Image Compressor – make JPEG, PNG and WebP photos smaller with a quality slider. See the original size, the new size and the percentage saved before you share.
• Target file size (Premium) – ask for "under 200 kB" and FilePilot finds the best quality that fits, and tells you honestly when a target can't be reached.
• Image Resizer – presets (640, 1280, 1920 px), percentage or custom width and height, with aspect-ratio lock. Images are never enlarged unless you ask.
• Image Converter – real conversion between JPEG, PNG and WebP (decoded and re-encoded, never just renamed). Transparency is kept in PNG and WebP.

**PDF tools**
• Merge PDFs – combine several PDFs and choose the order.
• Split PDF – by page ranges, every page, or every N pages.
• Extract pages – build a new PDF from the pages you pick.
• Image to PDF – turn photos and scans into one PDF (A4, Letter or fit to image, orientation, margins).
• PDF to Images – save pages as JPEG, PNG or WebP at 96, 150 or 300 dpi.
Merging, splitting and extraction copy pages as they are: text stays selectable and quality is unchanged.

**Private by design**
• Files are processed on your device and never uploaded to our servers.
• No storage or photo permission: you pick each file in Android's file picker.
• Your original files are never modified.
• Temporary copies are deleted automatically.
• Results stay in FilePilot's private storage until you delete them, or save a copy to any folder.
• Optional anonymous statistics, off by default, never include file names or contents.

**Free and Premium**
FilePilot is free to use with daily free operations and ads. Premium unlocks unlimited operations, batch compression, target file size, larger PDF jobs, full history and removes ads. Monthly, annual and lifetime options; prices are shown in your local currency in the app.

Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP images and PDF documents. Password-protected PDFs, HEIC, GIF, BMP and TIFF are not supported yet. PDF compression is not offered.

Available in English, French and Spanish.
