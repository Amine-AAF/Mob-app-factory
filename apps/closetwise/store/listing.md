# ClosetWise — store listing drafts

## en-US
**Title (30):** ClosetWise: Outfit Planner
**Short description (80):** Your digital wardrobe: organize clothes, build outfits and plan your looks.

**Full description:**
ClosetWise turns your closet into a beautiful, private digital wardrobe.

• Digital wardrobe — add clothes in seconds with a photo and a category. Add colors, brand, size, season,
  style, occasion, price and notes whenever you like. Search, filter, sort, grid or list view, favorites, archive.
• Outfit builder — combine tops, bottoms, dresses, jackets, shoes, bags and accessories and preview the look.
• Outfit ideas — get suggestions from the clothes you already own, by occasion and season, based on simple,
  transparent rules. Rate them, exclude pieces, edit anything.
• Outfit planner — plan your week and special events on a calendar, get an optional morning reminder.
• Wardrobe insights — categories, colors, seasons, brands, most and least worn items, estimated value and
  cost per wear.
• Shopping tracker & budget — log purchases in any currency, follow a monthly budget, compare periods.
• Wishlist — keep track of what you want, with priorities and links.
• Packing assistant — trip checklists with templates, planned outfits and "don't forget" checks.
• Laundry & care — clean, to wash, in laundry, to iron; save care instructions from the label.
• Goals & challenges — 7 days 7 outfits, no-shopping challenge, capsule wardrobe… at your own pace.

Private by design: no account, works offline, your photos stay on your phone. English, French and Spanish.
Free with up to 50 items; Premium unlocks an unlimited wardrobe, advanced suggestions, full insights,
advanced calendar and packing, export, premium themes and removes ads.
