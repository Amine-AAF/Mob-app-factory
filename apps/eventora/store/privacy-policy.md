# Eventora — Privacy policy (template to adapt and host publicly)

_Last updated: TO_FILL. Publisher: TO_FILL (legal name, address, contact e-mail)._

**Summary.** Eventora works without an account and stores your events and your guests' details only on your device. The publisher does not operate a server for Eventora and does not receive your data.

1. **Data you enter** (events, guests, contact details, dietary preferences, budgets, vendors, gifts, photos, documents) is stored locally in the app's private storage. It is not transmitted to the publisher or to third parties. You are responsible for having a legitimate reason to store details about your guests.
2. **Sharing.** Data leaves your device only when you share an invitation, a list or a report through your phone's share menu, to the recipient you choose. The app warns you before sharing lists that contain personal data.
3. **Contacts.** The app does not access your address book.
4. **Notifications** are scheduled locally and contain no guest names, contact details or amounts.
5. **Advertising (free version).** Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data according to your consent choices (Google UMP, where required). No guest data is shared with advertisers. Premium removes ads.
6. **Purchases** are processed by Google Play; RevenueCat receives purchase receipts to confirm your subscription. No payment card data is received.
7. **Analytics.** Off by default; when enabled, only anonymous usage events are recorded, with no personal data.
8. **Retention & deletion.** Data stays until you delete it: delete an event, use "Delete all my data" in Settings, or uninstall the app. Backups you export are files under your control.
9. **Children.** The app is not directed at children under 13.
