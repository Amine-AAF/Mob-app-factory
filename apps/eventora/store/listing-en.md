# Store listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤ 30):** Eventora: Event Planner

**Short description (≤ 80):** Plan weddings & parties: guests, RSVP, budget, seating and invitations. Offline.

**Full description:**

Eventora is your event planner and organizer, from the first idea to the final thank-you.

Plan a wedding, a birthday, a baby shower, a conference or a family dinner in one place. Each event gets its own dashboard with guests, RSVPs, budget, tasks, programme, seating plan and vendors.

★ GUEST LIST & RSVP
• Families and groups, adult companions and children counted automatically
• Dietary preferences and notes kept private on your phone
• Duplicate detection, search and filters
• Import a guest list from a CSV spreadsheet
• RSVP tracker: confirmed, declined, awaiting, people expected

★ INVITATIONS
• 24 elegant designs for weddings, birthdays, corporate and family events
• Colours, typefaces, layouts and your own background photo
• Share as PDF or text through WhatsApp, e-mail or any app — you stay in control

★ BUDGET
• Planned budget per category, estimated and actual expenses
• Payment status, due dates, receipts and invoices
• Multi-currency done right: amounts in different currencies are never mixed without a rate you enter

★ TASKS, PROGRAMME & SEATING
• 20 planning templates with ready-made checklists
• List and calendar views, overdue tasks, overall progress
• Hour-by-hour programme with overlap detection (after-midnight parties included)
• Visual seating plan: round, rectangular and head tables, families seated together

★ VENDORS, GIFTS & REPORTS
• Vendors with quotes, status, appointments and contracts
• Gifts and contributions with thank-you tracking
• Professional PDF and CSV reports, JSON backup

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. Works offline. Your data stays on your phone.
• Eventora never reads your contacts and never sends invitations on your behalf.
• Reminders never show guest names or contact details.

Free: up to 2 active events, 150 guests per event, essential tools.
Eventora Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime): unlimited events and guests, all invitation designs and templates, advanced seating, PDF & CSV exports, advanced budget and vendor management, no ads.

Prices are shown in Google Play in your local currency.
