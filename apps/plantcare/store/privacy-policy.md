## Summary

PlantCare works offline and has no account and no server. Your plants, photos, notes, care
history and settings are stored **only on your device**.

## Data stored on your device

- Plant collection, garden spaces, care schedule and history, growth records, journal entries,
  health notes, fertilizer and soil records, wishlist, seasonal checklists, preferences.
- Photos you take or pick are copied into the app's private storage. They are never uploaded,
  shared or analysed by PlantCare.
- Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. You can export a backup file yourself; you decide
  where it goes.

You can delete everything at any time: **Profile → Data & backup → Delete all my data**.
Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## Third-party services

- **Google AdMob** (free version): shows ads and may use the advertising ID and device information,
  according to your consent choices (Google User Messaging Platform where required). See
  https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- **Google Play Billing / RevenueCat** (only if you buy Premium): processes the purchase and
  verifies your subscription status. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

PlantCare does not sell personal data. Anonymous usage statistics are off by default; in
version 1.0 no analytics provider is included.

## Permissions

- Notifications (optional): local care reminders scheduled on the device.
- Camera (optional): only when you choose to take a photo of a plant.

## Children

PlantCare is not directed to children under 13.
