# BookNest — store listing

## English (en-US, default)
**Title (≤30):** BookNest - Reading Tracker
**Short description (≤80):** Your personal library, reading tracker, goals and quotes — private and offline.

**Full description:**
BookNest is a warm, elegant home for your books and your reading life.

📚 MY LIBRARY — Catalogue your books with covers, authors, genres, formats and notes. Grid or list, instant search, filters, favorites, archive and duplicate detection. Built for libraries of thousands of books.

📖 READING TRACKER — Want to read, reading, finished, on hold, abandoned. Update your page in a tap, see your progress, read a book again and keep the history. Audiobooks too.

⏱️ READING SESSIONS — Start a timer, pause, resume. It stays exact even if your phone puts the app to sleep. Or log a session by hand.

🎯 GOALS & CHALLENGES — Books per year, pages per month, minutes per day, reading days per week, plus challenges like 12 Books in a Year. Motivating, never guilt-inducing.

✍️ QUOTES, JOURNAL & REVIEWS — Save the passages you love, write impressions and ideas, rate and review privately, build your personal ranking.

📊 STATISTICS & CALENDAR — Books, pages, reading time, favorite genres and authors, records and an activity calendar. Honest numbers: nothing invented when data is missing.

🧭 DISCOVER — Ideas from your own library: favorite genres, forgotten wishlist books, unfinished reads, a pick of the day. Simple rules, no AI, nothing leaves your phone.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN — No account, no server. Your data stays on your device. Backup, CSV import/export, and delete everything anytime.

BookNest Premium: unlimited library and collections, advanced statistics, full history, custom goals, CSV & PDF export, themes, backups with covers, no ads.
