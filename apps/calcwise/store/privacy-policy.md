CalcWise ("the app") is published by [PUBLISHER NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what happens to
your data when you use the app. In short: **your calculations never leave your device.**

## 1. Data that stays on your device

- Calculation history (expressions, results and the inputs of the tool calculators), your shortcuts and
  your preferences (language, theme, number format, currency, default rates).
- This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We have no server and no cloud
  sync, so we cannot see, access or recover it. Android auto-backup is disabled for this app.
- You can delete single calculations, clear the history, or erase everything at any time in
  **Settings → Delete all my data**. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## 2. No account

The app has no sign-up and never asks for your name, email address or phone number.

## 3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and use your device's
advertising ID and information about your interaction with ads to show, measure and limit ads and to
prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK) the app asks for your consent
through Google's consent tool before ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in
**Settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android
settings. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Ads are never shown on the calculator keypad, inside a tool or over a result.

## 4. Purchases (Premium)

Premium is bought through **Google Play**. Purchases are verified using **RevenueCat** (a purchase
management service), which receives an anonymous app user identifier and your purchase receipts in
order to confirm whether Premium is active. We never receive your payment card details.
RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 5. Optional anonymous statistics

A switch in Settings ("Share anonymous usage statistics") is **off by default**. In version 1.0 no
statistics service is connected, so nothing is sent even if it is on. If a statistics service is added
in a future version, this policy will be updated first, and only events such as "a tool was opened"
would be counted — never numbers, expressions, amounts, results or your history.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## 7. Security

Network traffic from the advertising and purchase SDKs is encrypted (HTTPS). Data stored on the device
is protected by Android's app sandbox.

## 8. Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you on our side, requests regarding the data processed by
Google (ads) or RevenueCat (purchases) can be made to those companies directly. For any question about
this policy, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 9. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if our practices change.
