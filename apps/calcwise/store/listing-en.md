**Full description (≤ 4000):**

One calculator for everything you work out in a day.

CalcWise brings together the calculators you actually use — everyday maths, shopping, bills, loans, savings and conversions — each with its own clear, focused screen. No account, no internet needed, and your calculations stay on your phone.

STANDARD & SCIENTIFIC CALCULATOR
Large, comfortable keys, parentheses, percentages, negative numbers and a live preview of the result. Switch to scientific mode for powers and roots, sin, cos, tan and their inverses, log and ln, factorial, π and e, degrees or radians and scientific notation. Results are computed with high-precision decimal arithmetic, so 0.1 + 0.2 is exactly 0.3.

PERCENTAGE CALCULATOR
X % of a number, increase and decrease, the change between two values, what percentage one value is of another, the price before a discount, margin and markup.

DISCOUNT & SHOPPING
Final price and money saved, stacked discounts (−20 % then −10 % is −28 %, not −30 %), VAT added or removed at your rate, and a "best deal" comparison by price per kg, per litre or per piece.

TIP & BILL SPLIT
Add a tip, split the bill fairly between friends — leftover cents are shared out so the total always adds up — or round each share up to a convenient amount.

UNIT CONVERTER
Length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data storage (kB and KiB) and energy, using exact international definitions. See the value in every unit at once.

LOAN CALCULATOR
Monthly payment, total interest and total cost of a fixed-rate loan, with a year-by-year summary. Estimates for information only — not a credit offer or financial advice.

SAVINGS CALCULATOR
Compound interest with monthly deposits, monthly, quarterly or yearly compounding, and a chart of your progress. All assumptions are visible and editable.

HISTORY & SHORTCUTS
Every result is saved automatically on your device. Search it, copy a result, reuse it in the calculator, delete single entries or clear everything. Pin your favourite tools to the home screen.

MADE FOR YOU

• English, French and Spanish
• Number formats and currency that follow your region, or your choice
• Works fully offline
• No account, no cloud: nothing you type is sent anywhere
