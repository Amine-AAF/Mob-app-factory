# LearnSphere — Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: 2026-11-13. Replace the bracketed fields and host this page over HTTPS before release._

LearnSphere is a study planner that works on your device. We built it so your learning stays yours.

**What stays on your device.** Your subjects, courses, notes, flashcards, quiz results, study plans,
exams, goals, imported files and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
We do not operate a server that receives them, and the app never uploads them automatically.

**No account.** You don't need to sign up. An optional first name is stored locally to greet you.

**Backups.** You can export a backup file. It is created on your device and you decide where to keep
it (for example, in your own cloud drive). Restoring reads a file you choose.

**Advertising (free plan).** The free plan shows a banner from Google AdMob. Google may process your
device's advertising identifier and usage information to serve and measure ads, according to Google's
policies and the consent choices you make in the consent form shown where required by law. Your
notes, cards and results are never shared with advertising networks. Premium removes ads.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat
receives purchase information to confirm your Premium access. We don't receive your payment details.

**Usage statistics.** Off by default. If you turn them on, only anonymous events (such as "quiz
completed") may be collected — never the content you write.

**Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn each one off.

**Deleting your data.** Settings › Backup & data › Delete all my data erases everything on the device.
Uninstalling the app also removes it.

**Children.** LearnSphere is not directed at children under 13.
