# Store listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤ 30):** LearnSphere: Study Planner

**Short description (≤ 80):** Plan your studies, review with flashcards, quiz yourself and track progress.

**Full description:**

LearnSphere is your personal learning assistant: organise what you study, plan when you study it,
remember more and see your progress — all on your phone, no account needed.

📚 SUBJECTS & COURSES
Organise subjects into courses, chapters, modules and lessons. Add links and PDFs, mark chapters done
and watch your progress grow. Search, favorites and archive keep things tidy.

🗓 STUDY PLANNER
Plan sessions by day, week or month, repeat them every week, mark them done or move them to later.
Smart suggestions based on your exams, deadlines, goals and available time tell you what to study next.

📝 NOTES
Write clear summaries with headings, lists, highlights and key ideas. Tags, search and a version
history keep every note safe.

🧠 FLASHCARDS WITH SPACED REPETITION
Create your own cards and review them at the right time. Rate each card Forgot, Hard, Medium or Easy:
LearnSphere schedules the next review so you remember for longer.

✅ QUIZZES
Practise with 400 built-in questions (general knowledge, logic, math, science) or write your own for
each subject. Multiple choice, true/false, short answer and matching, with explanations, exam mode and timer.

⏰ EXAMS & DEADLINES
Countdowns, priorities and a preparation checklist for exams, tests, assignments, projects and certifications.

⏱ FOCUS MODE
Pomodoro 25/5, 50/10 or your own rhythm. Keeps counting in the background and tells you when to take a break.

📈 PROGRESS & GOALS
Study time per day and subject, streaks, quiz and flashcard results, daily/weekly/monthly goals,
personal challenges and badges — encouraging, never guilt-inducing.

🧩 24 TEMPLATES
University semester, exam preparation, language learning, certification, daily routine, reading plan
and more — customise one and get a complete plan in seconds.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Works offline. Your notes and results stay on your device and are never shared with advertisers.
Export, restore or delete your data any time.

Premium: unlimited subjects and flashcards, advanced spaced repetition, detailed statistics, note
export, all templates, personalisation and no ads.
