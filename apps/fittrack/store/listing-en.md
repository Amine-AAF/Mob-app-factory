# Play listing — English (en-US)

**App name (30):** FitTrack - Workout Planner

**Short description (80):** Plan workouts, log sets & rest, track records and progress. Offline, no account.

**Full description:**

FitTrack is your personal workout planner and training log. Build your own programs or start from 32 ready-made templates, log every set in a few taps, and watch your progress — all on your phone, without an account.

**PLAN YOUR TRAINING**
• Workout Builder: multi-day programs, sets, rep ranges, weight, duration, rest and notes
• 32 templates: full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs, strength, home, dumbbell only, bodyweight, cardio, mobility…
• Weekly routine and calendar (day, week, month): plan, move, postpone or skip a session, add rest days

**TRAIN WITH FOCUS**
• Active workout mode with big buttons, pre-filled weights and reps from last time
• Rest timer with ±15 s, sound and vibration, and an alert even when your phone is locked
• Your workout is never lost, even if the app closes unexpectedly

**219 EXERCISES**
• Original step-by-step instructions, coaching tips, common mistakes, easier and harder variations
• Search and filter by muscle group, equipment you have and level; favorites and recent exercises

**TRACK YOUR PROGRESS**
• Personal records, progression charts and an estimated 1RM (clearly labelled as an estimate)
• Cardio log: running, walking, cycling, rowing, swimming… with speed and pace calculated from your entries
• Body progress: weight and measurements, charts, comparisons and private progress photos
• Statistics: weekly frequency, training time, most practised exercises, muscle-group balance
• Goals, challenges and badges designed to motivate — rest days always count

**TOOLS**
Rest timer, stopwatch, countdown, interval, Tabata and EMOM timers — all offline.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
Your data stays on your device. No account. No body data sent to anyone. Export or delete it anytime.

**PREMIUM**
Unlimited programs, premium templates, advanced library, detailed statistics, full history, all body measurements and photos, advanced timers, CSV export and no ads. Monthly, yearly or lifetime.

FitTrack provides general fitness information and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.
