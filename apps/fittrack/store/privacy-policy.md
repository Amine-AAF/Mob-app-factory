# FitTrack — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13. To be hosted publicly and set as `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL`. Replace the
bracketed placeholders before publication._

FitTrack ("the app") is published by [Publisher name] ([contact email]).

## Your data stays on your device
FitTrack works without an account and without our own servers. Your programs, workouts, sets, personal
records, cardio activities, goals, body measurements, progress photos and settings are stored only on
your phone. We cannot see them.

## Health and fitness information
Body measurements, weights, performances and photos are optional. They are never sent to us, to analytics
providers or to advertising partners. Progress photos are kept in the app's private storage, are never
uploaded and are not included in backups.

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob on browsing screens only. Before any ad request, a
consent form is shown where required by law (Google User Messaging Platform). Ads are requested as
non-personalized and no fitness data is used for advertising. AdMob may process device identifiers and
diagnostics as described in Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
Premium removes ads.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat is used to check your
entitlement with an anonymous identifier. We do not receive your payment details.

## Analytics
Anonymous usage statistics are **off by default**. If you turn them on (Settings › Privacy), only event
names (e.g. "workout completed") are sent, never your values or notes.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and contain generic text only.

## Permissions
- Notifications: only if you enable reminders.
- Camera: only when you choose to take a progress photo. Gallery access uses the Android photo picker.
No location, contacts, microphone or storage permission is requested.

## Your choices and rights
You can export all your data (JSON), restore it, and delete everything from Profile › Data & backup.
Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. You can change ad consent in Settings › Privacy and reset
your advertising ID in Android settings. For any question: [contact email].

## Children
FitTrack is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
We will update this page and its date if this policy changes.
