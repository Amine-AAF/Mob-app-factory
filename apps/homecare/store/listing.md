# HomeCare — store listing

## en-US
**Title:** HomeCare: Home Maintenance
**Short:** Plan home maintenance, track equipment, warranties, documents and bills. Offline.
**Full:**
HomeCare keeps your whole home in one calm place — no account, no cloud, it works offline.

• Maintenance planner: recurring tasks, calendar, reminders and 60+ editable templates (filters, boiler, smoke detectors, gutters…).
• Equipment: brand, model, serial number, purchase price, warranty end and full repair history.
• Warranties & documents: keep invoices, manuals, contracts and insurance privately on your phone, with expiry reminders.
• Expenses: bills, recurring costs, monthly budget, charts — in your currency, never mixing currencies.
• Repairs & services: problems, technicians, costs, before/after photos.
• Chores: checklists, repeating tasks, optional household members.
• Home inventory: valuable items with photos and value for insurance or moving.

Your data stays on your device. Back up and restore whenever you want.
Premium: several homes, unlimited equipment and documents, full history, PDF/CSV exports, advanced reminders, no ads.
Suggested maintenance intervals are general presets, not technical advice — follow your manufacturer's instructions.
