# Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name (≤ 30):** DevSpeak: English for Devs  *(26 chars — "DevSpeak - English for Developers" is 33 and exceeds Play's limit)*

**Short description (≤ 80):**
Improve your technical English, vocabulary and interview skills.

**Full description (≤ 4000):**

Speak up in stand-ups. Explain your code with confidence. Answer interview questions in natural, professional English.

DevSpeak is an English course built specifically for software developers and tech professionals. Instead of generic phrases about travel and shopping, you learn the English you actually use at work: daily meetings, code reviews, architecture discussions, incident reports and job interviews.

WORKPLACE ENGLISH
Real situations from engineering teams: giving your stand-up update, reporting blockers, sprint planning and reviews, retrospectives, explaining a delay, negotiating deadlines, giving and receiving feedback, talking to managers and clients, writing clear chat messages and emails.

TECHNICAL ENGLISH
Learn to talk about Java, Spring Boot, React, JavaScript and TypeScript, REST APIs, microservices, Kafka and event-driven architecture, SQL and databases, Git, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, cloud, testing and debugging. Practise explaining a problem, a code change, an API, a database schema, a performance issue, a security vulnerability or a production incident.

IT INTERVIEW PREPARATION
Interview paths for Junior, Java, Spring Boot, React, Full Stack, DevOps, QA and Software Architect roles. Each topic explains what the interviewer is really checking, gives useful vocabulary, an example answer written in natural English, alternative expressions, a practice exercise and a self-assessment checklist.

TECH VOCABULARY & FLASHCARDS
A searchable dictionary of essential tech terms with clear definitions, example sentences, translations and audio pronunciation. Save words and review them with spaced-repetition flashcards.

SPEAKING & LISTENING PRACTICE
Listening exercises, sentence completion, role-play dialogues, read-aloud practice and interview answers. Record yourself and compare with the model audio. (DevSpeak does not automatically grade pronunciation.)

DAILY CHALLENGE & PROGRESS
One expression, one tech word, one mini-dialogue and one quiz every day. Track your streak, XP, level, weekly activity, learning calendar and achievements.

BUILT FOR YOU
• Personalized recommendations based on your level, role and goals
• Interface in English, French or Spanish — lessons always in English, with optional translations
• Works offline, no account required, your progress stays on your device
• Dark mode, adjustable audio speed, accessible text sizes
• Daily reminders you control

FREE AND PREMIUM
DevSpeak is free to use with selected lessons, the daily challenge, quizzes, part of the dictionary and progress tracking, supported by ads. DevSpeak Premium unlocks every lesson and interview path, the full dictionary, unlimited speaking practice, advanced flashcards and detailed statistics, without ads. Subscriptions are billed through Google Play and can be cancelled at any time.

All lessons are original content written for DevSpeak.
