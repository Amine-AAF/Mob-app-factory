## 1. Summary
DevSpeak is an English-learning app for software professionals. It works without an account.
Your learning data stays on your device. The free version shows ads; Premium is a subscription
sold through Google Play.

## 2. Data stored only on your device
- Learning profile (English level, role, goals, daily time, optional first name)
- Progress (lessons, quiz scores, streaks, XP, flashcard schedule, saved words, achievements)
- Preferences (language, theme, reminders)
- Voice recordings made during speaking practice (temporary app cache)

We do not receive this data. You can export it, restore it, or delete it at any time in
*Settings › Your data*. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 3. Data processed by third parties
**Advertising (free version) — Google AdMob.** Google may collect device identifiers (such as the
advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Where required
(e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool and you can
change it in *Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices*. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

**Subscriptions — Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.** Payments are handled by Google Play; we never
see your payment details. RevenueCat, Inc. receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts
to verify your Premium access. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

**Anonymous usage statistics (optional, off by default).** [Version 1.0 does not ship an analytics
service. If enabled in a future version, describe the provider, data and retention here.]

**Text-to-speech.** Audio is generated by your device's speech engine.

## 4. Legal bases (GDPR)
Consent (ads personalisation, optional statistics); performance of a contract (subscription);
legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention).

## 5. Your rights
You can access, export and delete your local data in the app. For data held by Google or
RevenueCat, use their tools or contact us at [PRIVACY EMAIL]. You may lodge a complaint with your
data protection authority.

## 6. Children
