# Play listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤ 30):** PetCare: Pet Health Tracker

**Short description (≤ 80):** Vet visits, vaccines, treatments, weight & reminders for all your pets. Offline.

**Full description:**

PetCare keeps everything about your pets in one warm, private place — on your phone, with no account.

🐾 PROFILES FOR EVERY PET
Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, fish, reptiles and more: photo, breed, birthday or approximate age, weight, ID number and adoption date.

🩺 HEALTH HISTORY
Record vet visits with the reason, vet, clinic, the diagnosis your vet gave you, exams, results and fees. Browse a clear medical timeline and attach documents.

💉 VACCINATIONS & TREATMENTS
Enter the dates your vet sets, see what is upcoming or overdue, and get reminders before boosters. Track ongoing treatments with dose reminders, a history of doses given and an end-of-treatment alert.

⚖️ WEIGHT & GROWTH
Log measurements and follow the evolution on a simple chart.

🍽️ FEEDING & FOOD STOCK
Meal schedules and reminders, food inventory, restock alerts and purchase history.

📅 APPOINTMENTS
Vet, grooming, training, boarding: date, address, provider and reminders.

📁 DOCUMENTS & PET PASSPORT
Vaccination records, prescriptions, invoices, test results, insurance… Import, categorise, search and share. Files stay on your phone.

🚶 DAILY CARE & EXPENSES
Walks with personal goals, play and grooming logs. Track spending by category, month and year with an optional budget.

✅ 34 CARE CHECKLISTS
New pet, puppy, kitten, travel, vet visit preparation, grooming routine, seasonal safety and more — all customisable.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no server, works offline. Your data is never uploaded. Export a backup or delete everything at any time.

⭐ PREMIUM
Unlimited pets, full history, unlimited documents, advanced statistics, PDF & CSV exports, all checklists and no ads.

PetCare is an organiser, not a medical device: it does not diagnose, prescribe or replace your veterinarian. Always follow your vet's advice.
