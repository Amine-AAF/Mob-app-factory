# Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name (≤ 30):** HabitFlow - Habit Tracker

**Short description (≤ 80):** Build better habits, track streaks and plan your day. Offline, no account.

**Full description:**

HabitFlow helps you build good habits, drop the ones you don't want and organize your days — one day at a time.

TODAY, AT A GLANCE
• Your daily progress with a clear progress dial
• One-tap check-ins, with a quick undo
• Habits done, habits left, and a short summary of your day
• A light planner for the day's one-off tasks

FLEXIBLE HABITS
• Every day, chosen weekdays, X times per week, X times per month or every few days
• Simple done / not done, amounts (glasses, pages, steps) or minutes with a daily target
• Icons, colors, categories, notes and an estimated duration
• Archive a habit without losing its history

STREAKS THAT ARE FAIR
• Current and best streak, completion rate, weekly and monthly consistency
• Days that aren't scheduled never break a streak
• Skip a day when life happens
• Changing a habit never rewrites your past statistics

CALENDAR & HISTORY
• Month and week views with colors for complete, partial and missed days
• Open any past day to see or correct it, and add daily notes

INSIGHTS FROM YOUR OWN DATA
• Daily, weekly and monthly rates, simple charts and week-over-week comparison
• Short observations such as "Reading is your most consistent habit"
• All statistics are calculated on your phone from your real check-ins

44 TEMPLATES
Morning routine, health, fitness, reading, learning, productivity, mindfulness, personal finance, lifestyle and
digital detox — customize any template before adding it.

GENTLE MOTIVATION
XP, levels and badges for real completions, a weekly goal and small celebrations — never more than one reward
per habit and day.

REMINDERS YOU CONTROL
Local reminders per habit or a daily check-in, on the days you choose. Each one can be turned off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline, no account, no HabitFlow server
• Export and import your data (JSON), or delete everything in one tap
• Light and dark themes, English, French and Spanish

PREMIUM (OPTIONAL)
Unlimited habits, the full template library, advanced statistics, extra color themes and no ads. Google Play shows
prices in your local currency. The free version stays complete for daily tracking.

HabitFlow is a personal organization tool. It does not provide medical or psychological advice.
