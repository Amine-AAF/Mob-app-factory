## Summary
HabitFlow is a habit tracker and daily planner that works offline and without an account. Your habits, check-ins,
notes, tasks and reminders are stored **only on your device**. There is no HabitFlow server.

## Data stored on your device
Habits and their settings, daily check-ins and notes, planner tasks, reminder times, achievements and app preferences.
This data is not transmitted to us. You can export it (JSON file you share yourself) or delete it at any time in
Settings › Backup & data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. No push-notification service is used.

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and usage data to
deliver and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy. Where required (e.g. EEA, UK), we ask for your consent
through Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy. Premium removes ads.

## Purchases
Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to verify your Premium status; RevenueCat receives an
anonymous app user identifier and your purchase receipts, not your habits or notes.

## Anonymous usage statistics
Off by default. If you turn it on and the feature is enabled in a future version, only anonymous events (such as
"a habit was created") are sent — never habit names, notes or your daily details.

## Children
HabitFlow is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children under 13.

## Your rights
Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: export, correct or delete it in the app. For questions
about ads or purchases, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

We will update this page and the date above if our practices change.
