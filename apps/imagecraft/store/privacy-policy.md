# ImageCraft — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13. Draft to be reviewed before publication; replace the contact address and host it at a public URL for the Play Console._

ImageCraft ("the app") is an offline photo editor. This policy describes what the app really does, including the third-party SDKs it contains.

## 1. Your photos
- Photos are chosen with the **Android photo picker**. The app can only open the photos you select; it has no access to the rest of your gallery, your camera or your location.
- All processing (crop, resize, compression, conversion, filters, text, watermarks, collages, batches) happens **on your device**. The app has no server and **never uploads your photos**.
- A private copy of imported photos and the results you create are stored in the app's private storage, so you can find, share or re-open them in *Projects*. You can delete any project, the whole history, or all data (*Settings → Delete all my data*). Temporary files are deleted automatically.
- Photos are sent elsewhere only when **you** choose *Share*, *Save to gallery* or *Export to a folder*.
- The app database stores only metadata (file names, sizes, formats, dates, edit settings) — never image pixels. App data is excluded from Android cloud backups.

## 2. No account
The app requires no account and collects no name, email address or phone number.

## 3. Advertising (free version)
The free version shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interaction data to show, measure and limit ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where required (e.g. EEA, UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change your choice in *Settings → Ad privacy choices*. **Ads never have access to your photos.** Premium users see no ads.

## 4. Purchases
Premium purchases are processed by **Google Play**. Entitlements are managed by **RevenueCat**, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts (no payment card details) — see https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. ImageCraft never sees your payment information.

## 5. Anonymous statistics (optional, off by default)
If you enable *Share anonymous usage statistics*, the app counts anonymous events (for example "image compressed", output format, coarse size bucket). These events never contain photos, file names, file paths, image metadata or any personal data. In the current version no analytics provider is configured: no statistics leave the device.

## 6. Permissions
The app requests **no** storage, media, camera, microphone or location permission. *Save to gallery* uses Android's MediaStore (Android 10+), which needs no permission for files the app creates. Internet access is used only by the AdMob and Google Play/RevenueCat SDKs.

## 7. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Data deletion
*Settings → Delete all my data* removes the history, every file created by the app and its settings from your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Copies you saved to your gallery or elsewhere are yours and are not affected.
