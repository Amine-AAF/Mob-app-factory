**App name (30):** ImageCraft - Photo Editor

## Full description (≤ 4000)

ImageCraft is a light, complete photo editor that prepares, edits and optimises your pictures directly on your phone — **without uploading them anywhere**.

**Edit. Resize. Create. Share.**

✂️ **Crop & rotate** — free crop or ratios (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9…), rotate, flip, undo and redo.
📐 **Resize** — pixels with ratio lock, percentage or presets (HD, Full HD, 4K…), with a size estimate.
🗜️ **Compress** — JPEG or WebP, adjustable quality, original vs. estimated size, exact result and savings after processing.
🔁 **Convert** — JPEG, PNG and WebP, with proper transparency handling.
🎨 **Filters & adjustments** — brightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, warmth, sharpness, blur, vignette, black & white, sepia, one-tap looks. What you see is what you export.
🔤 **Text & watermark** — captions with built-in fonts, colours, shadow, rotation; text or logo watermarks, placed or tiled.
📱 **Social media sizes** — Instagram post, portrait, story & Reel cover, TikTok, YouTube thumbnail & Shorts, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WhatsApp status — without ever stretching your photo.
🧩 **Collage maker** — 18 original layouts, spacing, rounded corners, background colour.
📚 **Batch processing** — compress, resize or convert many photos with the same settings.
🕘 **Projects** — find, share and re-open your recent work.

🔒 **Private by design**
• Everything is processed on your device. No account. No upload.
• ImageCraft only sees the photos you pick with the Android photo picker — no access to your whole gallery.
• Your original photo is never modified: every result is a new file.

💎 **Premium** (monthly, yearly or lifetime): all filters and effects, every social format, all collage layouts, custom watermarks, batches of up to 100 photos, high-resolution export and no ads.

Available in English, French and Spanish.
