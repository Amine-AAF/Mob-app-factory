# Listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤ 30):** SpendWise: Budget & Expenses

**Short description (≤ 80):** Track expenses, plan budgets and reach savings goals. Private, offline, no bank.

**Full description (≤ 4000):**

Understand your money. Take control.

SpendWise is a simple, private budget planner and expense tracker. Record your income and expenses, see where your money goes, and stay on budget — without connecting your bank account and without creating an account.

YOUR DASHBOARD AT A GLANCE
• Available balance calculated from your own accounts and transactions
• Income, spending and budget left for the month
• Latest transactions, top categories and savings goals
• Budget alerts when you reach 50%, 75%, 90% or 100%
• Privacy mode: hide every amount with one tap

TRANSACTIONS MADE EASY
• Expenses, income, transfers between your accounts, refunds and balance adjustments
• Categories, notes, payment method, optional time
• Search, filters, duplicate, edit and delete with confirmation
• Transfers between your own accounts are never counted as spending

ACCOUNTS & WALLETS
• Cash, bank account, savings, credit card, digital wallet or custom
• Each account with its own currency, colour, icon and history

BUDGETS THAT WORK
• Monthly budget and limits per category
• Spent, remaining and progress for every category
• Copy last month's budget in one tap — previous months are always kept
• Budget vs actual comparison

RECURRING & SUBSCRIPTIONS
• Salary, rent, bills and other recurring transactions — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or custom
• Each due date is recorded only once; planned items stay separate until they happen
• Subscription tracker with estimated monthly and yearly cost and upcoming renewals
• Optional local reminders

SAVINGS GOALS
• Emergency fund, new car, vacation, home…
• Progress, remaining amount and the monthly amount needed to reach your target date
• Contributions and withdrawals, fully traceable

REPORTS & CALCULATORS
• Spending and income by category, daily spending, monthly trend, savings rate
• Simple insights based only on your real data
• Calculators: monthly budget, savings goal, expense split, salary breakdown, debt repayment
• Results are estimates for planning, not financial advice

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
• Works offline, no account, no bank login
• Data stored only on your phone — no cloud sync
• CSV export and import with preview, full JSON backup & restore
• Delete all your data at any time
• English, French and Spanish · light and dark themes · many currencies (ISO 4217)

SPENDWISE PREMIUM (optional)
Unlimited accounts, category budgets, custom categories and savings goals, detailed reports with year view and comparisons, advanced export, and no ads. Prices are shown by Google Play in your currency.

SpendWise is a personal tracking and planning tool. It is not a bank and does not provide financial, investment or tax advice.
