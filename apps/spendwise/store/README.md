Positioning constraints (Google Play "Financial services" policy):
- SpendWise is a **personal tracking & planning tool**. It is not a bank, does not hold or move money,
  offers no loans, credit, trading or investment recommendations and gives no financial advice.
- When filling the Play Console **Financial features declaration**, select only
  "budgeting / personal finance management (no financial transactions)". Re-check the policy wording
  at publication time.
- Do not use words such as "bank", "banking", "invest", "advisor", "loan" in the title or short description.

The overall process is in [factory/documentation/release-checklist.md](../../../factory/documentation/release-checklist.md).
Publication is manual: no session publishes automatically.
