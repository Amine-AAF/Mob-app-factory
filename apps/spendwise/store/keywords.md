Primary intents: budget planner, expense tracker, money manager, spending tracker, budget app offline, monthly budget, savings goal, subscription tracker, bill reminder, personal finance.

Rules:
- Title: brand + 2 intents max ("Budget & Expenses"). No "bank", "banking", "invest", "loan", "advisor".
- Differentiators to repeat: **no bank connection**, **offline**, **private / data on device**, **no account**.
- Never claim "secure", "encrypted" or "bank-level security" (not implemented).
- No fake ratings, awards or "#1" claims. No competitor names.
