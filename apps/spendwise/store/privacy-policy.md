# SpendWise — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13 · Replace the bracketed placeholders before publishing and host this page at the URL set in `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL`._

SpendWise ("the app") is published by [PUBLISHER NAME], [CONTACT EMAIL]. This policy describes what the app actually does in version 1.0.

## 1. Your financial data stays on your device
Accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules, subscriptions, savings goals, exchange rates and settings are stored **only** in a local database inside the app's private storage on your phone. SpendWise has no server, no user account and no cloud synchronisation. We cannot see, access or recover your data.

- The app never asks for bank credentials and cannot connect to your bank.
- Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.
- Data is protected by Android's app sandbox. **The app does not add its own encryption** and does not offer a PIN/biometric lock in this version. Backup and CSV files you export are **not encrypted**: store them carefully.

## 2. Files you export
CSV exports and JSON backups are created on your device and handed to the Android share menu. You choose where they go (e.g. a file manager or a cloud drive you use); that destination's own privacy policy then applies.

## 3. Advertising (free version)
The free version displays ads from **Google AdMob** on a few screens that do not show your amounts. Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a consent form provided by Google is shown and you can change your choice in Settings › Privacy. AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad-interaction data under [Google's policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). **No amount, balance, transaction description, account name, category or other financial information is ever sent to AdMob.** Premium removes ads.

## 4. Purchases
Premium purchases are processed by **Google Play**. Subscription status is verified through **RevenueCat** (an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts). We do not receive your payment card details.

## 5. Anonymous statistics (optional, off by default)
If you enable "Share anonymous usage statistics", the app may record anonymous events such as "a budget was created". These events never include amounts, currencies of your transactions, account names, descriptions, custom category names, balances or history. Version 1.0 ships no third-party analytics SDK; if one is added, this policy will be updated first.

## 6. Notifications
Optional budget alerts and subscription reminders are **local** notifications scheduled on your device. Their text does not include amounts.

## 7. Your choices and deletion
You can export all your data (CSV / JSON) or permanently delete it at any time from Settings › Data & backup › Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

## 8. Children
SpendWise is not directed at children under 13.

## 9. Not financial advice
SpendWise is a personal tracking and planning tool. Calculations and insights are estimates and are not financial, investment, tax or legal advice.
