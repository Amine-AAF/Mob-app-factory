# Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name (30):** SafeVault: Password Manager
**Short description (80):** Offline password manager & secure notes. No account, no ads, no tracking.

**Full description:**

SafeVault keeps your passwords, private notes, payment cards and identity documents in an encrypted vault on your phone. No account. No server. No ads.

• Logins: usernames, passwords, websites and notes. Secure copy — the clipboard is cleared automatically.
• Password generator: strong random passwords or easy-to-type passphrases.
• Secure notes: recovery codes and private information, with simple formatting.
• Cards and identities: card details (never the CVV or PIN), passport, ID, driving licence, addresses.
• Categories, tags, favorites and fast search.
• Security check: find weak, reused and old passwords — computed on your device only.
• Auto-lock, optional fingerprint unlock, screenshot blocking.
• Encrypted backups you control: save them where you want and restore them after reinstalling.

How it works: your master password is never stored. It is turned into a key with Argon2id, and every item is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305 before it is saved.

Please note: if you forget your master password, your data cannot be recovered — by design. SafeVault does not check data breaches (that would require sending data to a server). SafeVault has not yet been audited by an independent security firm.

Free: 50 logins, 10 notes, generator, encrypted backups. Premium: unlimited items, detailed security audit, custom categories, extended password history and more.
