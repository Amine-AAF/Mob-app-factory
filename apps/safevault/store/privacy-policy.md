# SafeVault — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-13. Publish this page at the URL set in `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL` and in Play Console._

**Short version: what you store in SafeVault stays on your device. We cannot see it.**

1. **No account, no server.** SafeVault has no backend. Your vault (passwords, notes, cards, identities,
   categories) is encrypted on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone.
2. **Master password.** It is never stored or transmitted. We cannot reset it or recover your data.
3. **Backups.** Encrypted backup files are created only when you ask, and saved only where you choose
   (a folder or an app you pick in the share sheet). Their content is encrypted with your backup password.
4. **No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.** The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash
   reporting SDK.
5. **Purchases.** If you buy Premium, payment is handled by Google Play. Subscription status is checked
   through RevenueCat, which receives purchase information and an anonymous identifier — never vault content.
6. **Permissions.** Internet (purchases only), biometrics (optional unlock). No access to contacts,
   location, camera, microphone, photos or files beyond the backup file you explicitly pick.
7. **Deletion.** Settings › Security › Delete the vault erases all data on the device. Uninstalling the app
   also removes it.
8. **Children.** SafeVault is not directed at children under 13.
9. **Contact.** _Support e-mail to define before publication._
