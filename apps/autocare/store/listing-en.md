# Play listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤ 30)**: AutoCare - Car Maintenance

**Short description (≤ 80)**: Car logbook: maintenance, fuel, expenses, insurance & reminders. Private, offline.

**Full description (≤ 4000)**:

AutoCare is the digital logbook for your car, motorbike or van. Record every service, fill-up and
expense in seconds, know what's due next, and keep your paperwork organised — all on your phone,
without an account.

MAINTENANCE THAT FITS YOUR VEHICLE
• Log oil changes, filters, brakes, tires, battery, coolant, timing belt, A/C and any repair
• Garage, cost, parts replaced, invoices and photos attached to each service
• Your own schedules from the owner's manual or your mechanic: by date, by mileage, or both
• Clear status: up to date, due soon, overdue — with reminders before it's time
• Search and filter your full history

REAL FUEL CONSUMPTION
• Fill-ups and EV charging in seconds: two of quantity, price and total — the third is calculated
• Consumption in L/100 km, km/L, mpg (US/UK), kWh/100 km or mi/kWh
• Correct handling of partial fills and missed entries: no misleading figures
• Cost per km or mile, average price paid, monthly and yearly spending, trends

EVERY EXPENSE IN ONE PLACE
• Fuel, maintenance, repairs, insurance, inspection, parking, tolls, washing, tires, taxes, financing…
• Recurring expenses, monthly budget, breakdown by category
• Total cost of ownership and cost per kilometre
• Several currencies? Amounts are kept separate, never added up wrongly

INSURANCE, DEADLINES & DOCUMENTS
• Insurance policies with expiry alerts, renewals and payments
• Your own deadlines: inspection, annual tax, warranty, financing — rules vary by country, you set them
• Registration, insurance certificate, invoices and warranties as PDF or images, with expiry dates
• Files stay in AutoCare's private storage on your phone

TIRES, CHECKLISTS, COMPARISON & REPORTS
• Tire sets (summer/winter/all-season), positions, installation history, costs and reminders
• 33 checklists: routine checks, pre-trip, post-trip, seasonal, used-car inspection, EV and hybrid
• Compare costs and consumption between your vehicles (Premium)
• PDF and CSV reports, shared with the Android share sheet (Premium)
• JSON backup and restore (free)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no server: your data stays on your device
• Documents and photos are never uploaded
• No access to your storage, camera, location or contacts
• Your vehicle information is never sent to advertising or analytics services

AutoCare Premium (monthly, annual or lifetime): unlimited vehicles, full history and advanced
statistics, PDF/CSV reports, advanced document management, vehicle comparison, advanced
reminders, detailed cost tracking and no ads. Prices are shown in your local currency by Google Play.

Checklists and reminders are informational. Always follow your vehicle manufacturer's
recommendations and local regulations. Mileage-based reminders are estimates based on the readings you enter.
