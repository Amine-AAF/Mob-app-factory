# AutoCare — Privacy Policy (draft to host at a public HTTPS URL)

_Last updated: 2026-11-13. Replace `[PUBLISHER]` and `[CONTACT EMAIL]` before publishing._

AutoCare ("the app") is published by [PUBLISHER]. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## Your data stays on your device
Everything you enter — vehicles, plate, VIN, mileage, maintenance, fill-ups, expenses, insurance,
deadlines, tires, notes, checklists — and every document or photo you attach is stored **only on
your device**, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no AutoCare server. We cannot
see, access or recover your data.

## Files
Documents and photos are copied into the app's private folder when you choose them with the system
picker. They are never uploaded. Temporary export files (PDF/CSV/backup) are deleted after sharing.

## Backups
You may export a backup file and share it wherever you choose; that file is then under your control.
Android cloud backup of the app is disabled.

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob on some overview screens. AdMob may collect
device identifiers (advertising ID) and usage information to deliver and measure ads, subject to
your consent where required (Google's consent form, available again in Settings → Privacy).
AutoCare never sends your vehicle information to AdMob. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat receives a purchase
token and an anonymous app user ID to verify your entitlement. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Analytics
Anonymous usage statistics are **off by default** and never include vehicle data, amounts or notes.

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

## Deleting your data
Settings → Backup & data → Delete all my data erases everything from the device. Uninstalling the
app also removes all data.

## Children
AutoCare is intended for adults (vehicle owners) and is not directed at children.
