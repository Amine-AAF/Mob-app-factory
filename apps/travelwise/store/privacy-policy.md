## Summary

TravelWise is a trip organiser that works offline. You don't need an account. Your trips,
itinerary, bookings, expenses, documents, photos and journal are stored **only on your device**.
We do not run a server that receives them.

## Data stored on your device

- Trips, destinations, itinerary, activities, bookings (including references you type), places,
  checklists, packing lists, weather notes, expenses, exchange rates and journal entries, in a
  local database.
- Travel documents (PDF, images) and photos you add, copied into the app's private storage.
  The app does not encrypt them itself: they are protected by your device's security (screen lock,
  Android device encryption). An optional in-app lock can require your fingerprint, face or
  screen lock to view documents.
- App preferences (language, theme, default currency).

This data is not sent to us or to third parties. It is excluded from Android's automatic cloud
backup. If you share an export (PDF itinerary, CSV of expenses) with another app, that app's own
privacy policy applies to the copy you shared.

You can delete all of this data at any time in **Settings → Delete all my data**. Uninstalling
the app also removes it.

## Third-party services

- **Google AdMob** (free version only): shows ads. It may collect device identifiers such as the
  advertising ID, and ad interaction data, according to the consent choices you make in the
  consent form shown where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland). You can change these choices in
  Settings. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Billing and RevenueCat** (only if you buy Premium): process and verify your
  purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information. It
  receives none of your trip content. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **Analytics**: off by default. If you enable "Share anonymous usage statistics", only anonymous
  events (for example "trip created") would be sent, never names, places, amounts, references,
  documents or photos. Version 1.0 ships no analytics provider.

## Permissions

- Notifications: only to show the task reminders you set.
- Biometrics: only if you turn on the documents lock.
- Internet: used only for ads and purchases. The app's features work offline.
- The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage.
  Files and photos are chosen by you with Android's pickers.

## Children

TravelWise is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
