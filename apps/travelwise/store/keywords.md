# ASO — search intents and rules

Google Play has no keyword field: ranking comes from the title, short description and full
description. Use each intent naturally, at most a few times, and never keyword-stuff.

| Intent (en) | FR | ES | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| trip planner | planificateur de voyage | planificador de viajes | title, short, full |
| travel itinerary | itinéraire de voyage | itinerario de viaje | short, full |
| travel organizer | organisateur de voyage | organizador de viajes | full |
| packing list | liste de bagages | lista de equipaje | full |
| travel budget / expense split | budget voyage / partage des dépenses | presupuesto de viaje / repartir gastos | full |
| offline travel app | appli voyage hors ligne | app de viaje sin conexión | short, full |
| booking organizer | réservations | reservas | full |
| travel journal | carnet de voyage | diario de viaje | full |
| currency converter offline | convertisseur de devises | conversor de divisas | full |

Rules:
- Never use competitor brand names, airline or hotel brand names.
- Do not promise bookings, live prices, live exchange rates, live weather or GPS navigation.
- Category: **Travel & Local**. Tags to pick in Play Console: Trip planner, Travel guide (if
  offered), Productivity is not appropriate as primary.
- Localize screenshots captions (see screenshots.md) rather than translating keywords literally.
