# Store listing — English (en-US)

**App name (≤ 30):** TravelWise: Trip Planner

**Short description (≤ 80):**
Plan trips offline: itinerary, bookings, budget, packing lists and documents.

**Full description (≤ 4000):**

Plan Your Trip. Enjoy Every Moment.

TravelWise keeps everything about your trip in one place, on your phone, even without Internet. No account needed.

PLAN YOUR TRIPS
• Create trips with one or several destinations, dates, travellers and a budget
• See what's next at a glance: countdown, today's plan, upcoming bookings, preparation progress
• Duplicate a trip to reuse its plan, archive past trips, keep your travel history

DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY
• Build your itinerary day by day on a visual timeline
• Add activities with times, address, price, notes and links
• Move activities between days and cities, reorder them, mark them as done
• Times stay in the destination's local time and never shift when your phone changes time zone
• Start from editable templates: weekend, city trip, road trip, family, business, beach

BOOKINGS IN ONE PLACE
• Save flights, hotels, trains, buses, car rentals, ferries, restaurants and activities
• Keep references, times and addresses at hand, in chronological order
• Everything is entered by you: TravelWise does not book anything and does not connect to providers

BUDGET & SHARED EXPENSES
• Set a budget and record expenses in any of 11 currencies
• See what's left, your biggest category and your average daily spending
• Split costs between travellers and get suggested reimbursements (no payment is made)
• Offline currency converter with the exchange rate you got, saved with its date

PACKING & CHECKLISTS
• Packing lists from templates (weekend, business, beach, winter, family, international) and 100+ ideas
• Pre-departure checklist with due dates and local reminders
• Weather notes you enter yourself to get packing suggestions

DOCUMENTS, PLACES & JOURNAL
• Keep tickets and booking confirmations (PDF or images) available offline
• Optional lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Save restaurants, hotels and sights, and open them in your map app
• Write a travel journal with your photos

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your trips, documents and photos stay on your device
• No account, no cloud sync, nothing uploaded
• Delete all your data at any time

PREMIUM (optional)
Unlimited active trips, detailed budget, custom expense splits, PDF itinerary and CSV expense exports, all templates, unlimited documents, and no ads. Prices are shown in your local currency by Google Play.

Visa, passport and entry requirements change: always check with official authorities. TravelWise does not provide official travel information.
