# Store listing — English (en-US)

**Title (≤ 30):** MealWise: Meal Planner & List

**Short description (≤ 80):** Plan your week of meals and get a smart grocery list. Works offline.

**Full description:**

MealWise turns "what are we eating this week?" into a plan, and the plan into a grocery list that is
actually right.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for every day, in a list or calendar view
• Move or duplicate a meal, copy a whole day or a whole week
• Ready-made plans: Family, Budget Friendly, Quick & Easy, Vegetarian, Student and more
• Save your favourite weeks as your own menus

A GROCERY LIST THAT ADDS UP
• Generated from your plan in one tap, grouped by aisle
• Identical ingredients are combined only when their units are compatible — never apples and oranges
• Skips what you already have in your pantry
• Add your own items, note estimated prices, check items off as you shop
• Share the list with your household through any app

100+ ORIGINAL RECIPES
• Breakfasts, lunches, dinners, snacks and desserts, vegetarian and vegan options, quick meals
• Search by recipe or ingredient, filter, favourites
• Create your own recipes or paste one as text to import it
• Adjust servings: quantities are recalculated, in metric or US units

COOKING MODE
• One step at a time in large text, ingredient check-list, built-in timers, screen stays on

PANTRY & LESS WASTE
• Track what's in the fridge, freezer and cupboard, with expiry reminders
• See which ingredients are missing for a recipe

NUTRITION & BUDGET
• Estimated calories and macros per serving, per day and per week, clearly marked as estimates
• Weekly food budget and spending tracker

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account. Your recipes, plans, lists and pantry stay on your phone
• Works fully offline · Backup, export and delete your data anytime
• English, French and Spanish

MealWise Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) unlocks all recipes and exclusive plans, unlimited
planning, advanced pantry ("what can I cook?"), weekly nutrition insights, budget history, exports and
removes ads. The free version stays genuinely useful.

Nutrition figures are estimates for general information and are not medical or dietary advice.
