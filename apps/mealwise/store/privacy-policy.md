## 1. Summary
MealWise works without an account and keeps your content on your device. We do not operate any server
that receives your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, pantry, prices or budget.

## 2. Data stored on your device
Recipes you create, planned meals, grocery lists, pantry items and dates, prices and expenses, favourites,
history and settings are stored locally (SQLite / app storage). They are not accessible to us. Android
backup of app data is disabled; you can create your own backup file from Settings › Your data.

## 3. Data processed by third parties
- **Advertising (free version):** Google AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, IP
  address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (e.g. EEA, UK,
  Switzerland, some US states), you are asked for consent through Google's consent tool first and can
  change your choice in Settings › Privacy. No content you enter is shared with advertisers.
  Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Purchases:** Premium purchases are processed by Google Play. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app
  user id and purchase receipts to manage your entitlement. RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
- **Anonymous statistics (optional, off by default):** if enabled, coarse usage events (for example
  "a meal template was used") may be collected without any content you typed.

## 4. Notifications
Reminders and timers are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

## 5. Your rights
You can export or delete all your data at any time in the app. For data processed by Google or
RevenueCat, see their policies. For any question: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 6. Children
MealWise is not directed to children under 13.

## 7. Health information
Nutrition figures in MealWise are estimates for general information. MealWise is not a medical device
and does not provide medical or dietary advice.

## 8. Changes
We will update this page and the date above when this policy changes.
