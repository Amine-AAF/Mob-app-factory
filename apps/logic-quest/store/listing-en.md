## Full description (≤ 4000)

Train your brain. Challenge your logic.

Logic Quest is a collection of original logic puzzles designed for short, satisfying sessions. Every puzzle has exactly one correct answer — and after each one, you see why.

FOUR KINDS OF CHALLENGES
• Number Sequences — find the hidden rule: steady steps, Fibonacci-style chains, alternating tracks, growing gaps, squares, primes and more.
• Pattern Recognition — shapes, colors, rotations, 3×3 grids, visual analogies and symmetry.
• Logical Deduction — ordering puzzles, "who has what" grids, if-then reasoning, truth-tellers and liars, code locks and word problems.
• Memory Challenge — light trails, flashing grids, number recall (forwards and backwards) and snapshot memory.

A NEW DAILY CHALLENGE
One fresh puzzle every day, the same for everyone on that date. It works offline.

TIMED OR RELAXED — YOUR CHOICE
Play at your own pace, or race the clock in 30-second, 1-minute or 3-minute challenges. The clock pauses while you read explanations, and timers are never required.

UNDERSTAND EVERY ANSWER
After you answer, Logic Quest explains the solution step by step: the rule of the sequence, the exact calculation, which clue rules out which option. Hints are there when you are stuck.

PROGRESS THAT MEANS SOMETHING
Earn XP, level up from Novice to Grandmaster, keep your streak alive and unlock achievements such as Pattern Master, Number Expert and Daily Challenge Champion. Difficulty rises gradually: medium and hard puzzles unlock as you solve.

DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE
• Light and dark themes
• Color-blind-friendly palette, and every symbol has a spoken description
• Large touch targets, a reduced-motion option and relaxed memory timing
• English, French and Spanish

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up. Your progress stays on your device; export it or delete it at any time.

FREE AND PREMIUM
The free version includes all four categories at easy and medium difficulty, the daily challenge, timed challenges and 3 free hints a day, with ads. Logic Quest Premium adds every hard puzzle, unlimited hints, advanced statistics, the Mixed Gauntlet and 3-minute timed games, and removes ads. You can also remove ads only, with a one-time purchase.
Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Google Play.
