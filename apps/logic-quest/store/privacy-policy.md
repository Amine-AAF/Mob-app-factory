## 1. Summary
Logic Quest is a puzzle and brain-training game. It works without an account. Your game data stays
on your device. The free version shows ads; Premium is a subscription sold through Google Play, and a
one-time "Remove ads" purchase is also available.

## 2. Data stored only on your device
- Game history (puzzles answered, results, hints used, time spent per puzzle)
- Progress (XP, level, streaks, daily challenges, achievements, statistics)
- Preferences (language, theme, accessibility options, reminder time)

We do not receive this data. You can export it, restore it, or delete it at any time in
*Settings › Privacy & data*. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 3. Data processed by third parties
**Advertising (free version) — Google AdMob.** Google may collect device identifiers (such as the
advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Where required
(e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool, and you can
change it in *Settings › Privacy & data › Ad privacy choices*. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
Ads are not shown to Premium users or to buyers of "Remove ads".

**Purchases — Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.** Payments are handled by Google Play; we never
see your payment details. RevenueCat, Inc. receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts
to verify your access. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
